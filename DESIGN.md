## Overview

This system applies to the downloadable **GuateGeeks institutional proposal PDF**. The browser simulator keeps its existing UI. The PDF is an A4 educational prospectus: it opens with the classroom experience, lets a reader identify each kit and its selected grades, explains the services, and ends with the financial decision.

## Colors

Deep plum frames the cover and final investment. GuateGeeks cyan identifies learning and programs; warm orange highlights a few moments of action. Warm paper keeps tables calm. Body and financial text use dark ink on light ground.

## Typography

Large display type carries the slogan and final investment. Program names use a distinct section scale. Body copy and grade tables remain legible at A4 print size. Small labels explain data rather than decorate headings.

## Layout

Use a 36pt page margin. Classroom photography dominates the cover; each kit has a paired classroom/product view. Group statistics and selected grades stay adjacent. Services use editorial rows with consistent vector icons. A running folio gives page position. The final page places the amount without VAT first, then VAT, total with VAT, group breakdown, and payment terms. Extra investment detail pages precede the final total.

## Shapes

Rectangular bands and precise rules echo printed educational prospectuses. Avoid rounded cards, decorative glows, and boxes inside boxes.

## Components

- **Program spread:** name, classroom photograph, kit photograph, equipment and student counts, selected grades.
- **Service list:** icon, included item, concise explanation; ownership and Socrates availability are explicit.
- **Optional projection:** suggested monthly charge, estimated revenue, GuateGeeks payment, and difference before the school's own expenses; never for INTEGRA.
- **Financial close:** total without VAT in the largest type; VAT, total with VAT, payment timing, and quote dates remain visible.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do preserve the supplied logo, the motto «Imagina, diseña y crea», every configured grade, and the existing calculations. Do count shared kits once. Do use photographs to explain the experience and identify the kit. Don't imply conceptual photos document a class at the quoted institution. Don't present separately priced services as included or an optional projection as net profit.
